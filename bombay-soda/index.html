How to enjoy

Chill. Pop. Sip slowly.

Chill it properly

Bombay Soda tastes best ice-cold. Keep a few bottles in the fridge before the meal starts.

Pop it open

Every 600 mL bottle serves two. Masala sodas come with masala and lemon to add as you like.

After a hearty meal

Biryani, thali, chaat or kebabs: finish the feast with Kashmiri or Pudina Masala Soda.
