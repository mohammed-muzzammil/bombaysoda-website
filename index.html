Our story

A family business, making soda since 1979.

We started in 1979 as Bombay Cold-Drinks, the BCD in our logo. The idea was simple: the tangy, spiced soda people love after a good meal deserves to be made properly, hygienically and the same way every time.

Today the family makes eleven Bombay Soda flavours and B-Spring drinking water, for shops, restaurants, events and homes.

Every bottle carries our family’s name, so every bottle has to be right. The Bombay Soda family
